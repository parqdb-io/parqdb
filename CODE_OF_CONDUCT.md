# Code of Conduct

Relify is committed to an open, welcoming, and professional community for
everyone who participates, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
ability.

## Expected Conduct

Participants are expected to:

- communicate with respect and empathy;
- give and receive constructive technical feedback;
- disagree with ideas without attacking people;
- acknowledge mistakes and correct them; and
- prioritize the health of the project and its community.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or attention, trolling,
personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information without
permission are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, community
channels, events, and public interactions made while representing Relify.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer at
`petrizhang@outlook.com`. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue.
Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and with respect for the reporter's
privacy.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue a
warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior
violates this policy. Enforcement decisions will consider the severity,
context, and pattern of the conduct.

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
